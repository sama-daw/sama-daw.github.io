Overview

The problem

Eva Pharma's Q3 2024 sales data (July–September) came from two merged sources — point-of-sale (POS) and invoicing (IS) systems — across hundreds of branches. The raw export was inconsistent by design: duplicate rows, mixed date formats, Arabic/English product and branch naming clashing in the same columns, numeric fields stored as text, and currency strings mixed into what should have been pure numbers. The goal was to clean it into something trustworthy enough to answer real business questions: which products and branches drive revenue, how did sales move across the quarter, and how did Sama's home region, Banha, perform.

Data Cleaning

From raw export to analysis-ready

The bulk of the work was cleaning, not charting. Here's what that actually involved:

  • Identified and dropped 10,001 fully duplicated transaction rows.
  • Filled missing Customer IDs by backfilling from matching Customer Name + Customer Address pairs, then dropped the small remainder that still couldn't be resolved.
  • Backfilled missing Customer Name and Customer Address values from other rows sharing the same customer ID, using the same logic for District Name via District Code.
  • Filled remaining unresolvable location gaps (District Name, Brick Name) with an explicit "Unknown" category rather than dropping more rows.
  • Normalized a Date column that mixed multiple string formats into proper datetime values, filling the small number of missing entries with the column's mode.
  • Qty arrived as a mix of integers and text (some with stray spaces or decimal points) — coerced the whole column to numeric.
  • Value mixed plain numbers with strings like "86.40 EGP" — stripped the currency suffix and whitespace, then converted to numeric.
  • Trans. Type had the same categories written multiple ways (فاتورة / " فاتورة" / INVOICE / Faturra) — trimmed whitespace and mapped every English/misspelled variant to its Arabic canonical label, collapsing 10 raw values into 3 real categories.
  • Product Name mixed Arabic ("الصنف N") and English ("Product N") labels for the same product line, plus inconsistent casing and stray spaces — normalized casing/whitespace and mapped every "Product" variant to the Arabic "الصنف" naming convention with a regex, case-insensitive.
  • Branch Name had 531 "unique" values that were really ~179 branches, duplicated by extra whitespace, inconsistent Arabic letter forms (أ/إ/آ → ا, ة → ه, ى → ي at word end), and known typos/abbreviations (e.g. unifying ك.ع. to ك.عملاء). Cleaning this alone cut branch count by two-thirds.
  • Dropped the redundant Source column once its information was no longer needed post-merge.
Analysis

What the clean data showed

Monthly trend

Revenue and quantity sold tracked together across July–September 2024, giving a clear read on the quarter's momentum going into September.

Top products

Ranked all products by total revenue and by units sold — the two rankings don't fully overlap, pointing to a mix of high-volume and high-value items.

Transaction mix

After standardizing transaction types, regular invoices (فاتورة) dominate transaction volume over credit notes and sample invoices.

Branch performance

Ranked the (now properly deduplicated) 179 branches by revenue to surface the top 10 performing locations.

Key accounts

Identified the top 10 customers by total purchase value — the accounts worth protecting and prioritizing.

Banha deep-dive

Filtered every branch/district/address field for Banha (in Arabic and English spellings) to isolate the region, then tracked its monthly trend — including a September spike — plus its own top products and top customers.
